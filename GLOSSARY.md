# Novel's portfolio

The portfolio site and the tools around it that Novel uses to win full-time roles, freelance clients and speaking work.

## Language

**Tailored link**:
A link to the site made for one employer or client, which shapes what they see (CV, projects, mode, language) and counts what they do.
_Avoid_: Variant link, CV link

**Rate card**:
The prices a tailored link offers one client, shown only on links made with prices.
_Avoid_: Price list, quote

**Proposal**:
The videos Novel records for one client and sends on that client's tailored link, with an optional note and a way to reply.
_Avoid_: Pitch, video page

**Proposal video**:
One titled video in a proposal, hosted on YouTube as an unlisted video.
_Avoid_: Clip, chapter

**Viewing**:
Everything one visit did with a proposal's videos, from the first play to leaving the page; one client can make several viewings on the same tailored link.
_Avoid_: Session (that is PostHog's whole visit and its replay), watch, view
